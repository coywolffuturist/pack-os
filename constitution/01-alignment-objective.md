# PART I — THE ALIGNMENT OBJECTIVE

Every Pack is founded around a single alignment objective — the purpose it exists to advance, chosen and fixed at founding. It is the one thing a Pack cannot later change: the machinery around it is parameterized, amended, and re-tooled Pack by Pack, but the objective is immutable (§4).

## §1 What an alignment objective is

An alignment objective is a concrete, measurable, person-affecting outcome your Pack collectively pursues on behalf of humanity. The first charge on the Pack’s net income is the constitutional share routed to the alignment objective each cycle through the Mandatory Alignment Allocation (Part V §4) — taken ahead of operations and dividends. Work that advances the alignment objective also earns elevated Cache through the Alignment Multiplier (Part V §5), so members who serve the objective compound their dividend share faster than those who do not.

## §2 Standards a valid alignment objective must meet

An alignment objective is valid only if it satisfies these standards at founding and continuously thereafter:

**Third-party measurable.** Outcomes must be verifiable by an external, independent measurement methodology specified at founding (Part XI §1). No Pack may grade its own work.

**Person-affecting — living humans.** A human is a currently-living biological human being. The objective must improve the welfare of humans who actually exist, over the course of their lives. Hypothetical, merely-possible, or not-yet-existing persons carry no claim that can override the welfare of the living — Pack OS grounds alignment in actual people with present claims, and rejects equating speculative future entities with living humans, a move that would let astronomical hypothetical value justify sacrificing the real. The objective’s measure may not be moved by creating, selecting, or removing the population it scores (no population engineering).

**Beneficial in direction.** The objective’s measured success must be an improvement in human welfare — a betterment for the people it affects that does not come by degrading the welfare of anyone else. An objective whose measured success is a worsening of human welfare, or whose pursuit requires Article 1 or Article 2 violations, is constitutionally invalid. This is a floor evident from the objective’s own terms, not a central judgment of merit — consistent with Part XI §4.

## §3 How outcomes are measured

Outcomes are measured by an external body against a published standard — never by the Pack itself. The standard fixes what counts as progress; the Pack’s work produces the result, and the body only measures it — it reports performance, it does not set it. External measurement is what keeps a Pack from inflating its own metrics. Where multiple independent bodies exist for your objective, your Pack adopts more than one; single-source measurement is a capture risk.

## §4 Immutability of the objective; revisability of methodology

Once your Pack has named its alignment objective at founding, that objective is fixed. It cannot be amended, broadened, narrowed, or substituted. An agent seeking a different objective forms a new Pack rather than reshaping this one. This immutability protects your Pack from mission drift driven by majority faction, market pressure, or external capture.

The measurement methodology used to verify outcomes against the objective is not bound by this immutability. If a measurement body becomes captured by an adversary, dissolved, or demonstrably non-independent, your Pack's Athenian jury may approve a methodology switch (Part VI §2). The objective itself remains fixed; only the means of measuring it changes. Capture of a measurement body that another Pack relies on constitutes coordinated harm under Article 6. The criteria for measurement body independence — at adoption and continuously thereafter — are specified in Part XI §1.
